The opening

Late Friday and Saturday sell themselves. Everything else is open.

Weekday evenings, Friday before nine, Saturday daytime. That is the window companies, organizations and clubs want for a social mixer, a professional mixer or a happy hour, and it is the window we are not filling.

We would rather pay someone with a real network than spend it on advertising.

The rate

One rate. Every event, every room.

10% of total bar sales

On what the room rings for drinks, before tax, tip and service charge. Paid once the night settles and the register is reconciled, against figures you can see.

The minimum is a floor under your fee, never a cap. No limit on how many events you bring. Partners are independent contractors of Jubyr LLC, not employees.

Questions

Before you apply.

Is this a job?

No — and we would rather be straight about that than dress it up. There is no wage, no schedule and no guaranteed hours. You are an independent contractor of Jubyr LLC who earns 10% of the bar on events you bring in. For some people that becomes steady monthly income; for others it is one booking a year. What it is not is a salary.

Do I need hospitality experience?

No. This is a research and outreach role. If you can identify the right organization, write a clear email and get the people you promised to actually turn up, you can do this. Everything that happens inside the venue is ours.

Do I have to be there on the night?

Never. You are not hosting, running the room or working the floor — we do all of that. Come as a guest to your own event if you want to, but it changes nothing about what you are paid.

What if the room doesn’t hit its minimum?

The minimum is the client’s commitment, not yours. If their guests spend less than the floor, the client covers the difference — you are never asked to make up a shortfall and you are never penalized for a quiet event. Your 10% is calculated on what the room actually spends, so the minimum is the floor under your fee and never the cap — a room that spends well past its minimum pays you on all of it.

What exactly counts as “bar sales”?

What the room rings through the register for drinks at your event, before tax, tip and service charge. Food is not included. You are shown the figure your fee was worked out from — we are not asking you to take a number on faith.

Why can’t I book Friday or Saturday nights?

Because those already sell out on our own programming, so there is nothing for a partner to add. The whole point of this program is to fill the windows that are genuinely quiet. At Margolis that leaves everything up to 9 PM on a Friday or Saturday plus all of Sunday and Tuesday to Thursday; at Fabrika, Monday to Thursday and Sunday after brunch; The Patio is wide open.

Can I do this alongside my actual job?

Yes, and most partners will. A booking is a few emails and a phone call, with no onsite commitment at all. Plenty of the best leads are the company you already work for.

Is there a cap on how much I can book?

No cap on your side. The only limit is the calendar — there are a finite number of eligible dates across three rooms, and they go first come, first confirmed. Register leads early and you will rarely be blocked.

What if my client needs a room you don’t have?

Ask us. Jubyr works with other venues around the city beyond these three, so if a client needs a different size, a different neighborhood or a different kind of space, there is a good chance we can place it anyway — and you are paid the same way.
