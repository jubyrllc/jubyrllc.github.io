Live Music

Hi there,

Thank you so much for reaching out about performing at Margolis.

I want to be fully transparent about how we operate, so expectations are clear from the start. Margolis primarily functions as a venue, not a traditional promoter. As a result, artists who perform here are responsible for attracting nearly 100% of their audience and shouldering the burden of advertising and promotion for their event.

In return, we structure compensation in a way that directly rewards you for bringing people through our doors:

  • You keep 100% of your presale revenue — every artist, every time, never split
  • Door revenue is split 50/50 for a first booking or a one-off night; once your event returns on a recurring basis, you receive 100% of the door
  • You receive 10% of eligible net venue sales on a ticketed event, or 15% on a free event
  • 50% off your own food and drink during the event
  • All food and beverage sales count toward your minimum, and we offer craft cocktails and bar food that help drive guest spend

Every booking carries a sales minimum set by your date, your hours, and whether the kitchen is open — from $1,500 for a Sunday–Thursday night up to $5,000 for a first Friday or Saturday 9 PM–2 AM slot. If sales fall below your minimum, you cover the difference and no venue-sales percentage is paid, so we can still fairly pay bartenders, security, and support staff. The exact minimum for your date is calculated in the form.

The full breakdown of minimums and payouts is on our site, so nothing here is a surprise later.

If this structure works for you, please complete our booking form. Once the form is received, I’ll give you a call to walk through the additional details and ensure everything is aligned. You can also schedule a call anytime, and I will reach out when you are available.

Looking forward to learning more about your vision.

Best,

Couch Reservation

Margolis is very much a pull-up-and-party type of place, no frills, just great vibes and ambiance. The closest we come to traditional “sections” is our couches, though we intentionally steer clear of exclusivity-driven party culture.

Couches are $200 each, available on a first-come basis, and seat up to 6 people with admission included. If you’d like to guarantee one for the night, you can reserve one in advance — the fee is paid at the time of booking, and the couch is held once payment goes through.

Please note that a couch reservation provides priority, comfortable seating in our lounge, but does not include a dedicated server. All orders are placed directly at the bar. You’ll simply have your own cozy space to enjoy throughout the evening.

Alternatively, we do sell bottles, and any bottle purchase includes a complimentary couch for your group. Please note that this is not traditional ‘bottle service,’ but an option to purchase a bottle as a cost-effective alternative to ordering individual shots and cocktails.

Let me know how you’d like to proceed or if you have any questions. I’m happy to help.
