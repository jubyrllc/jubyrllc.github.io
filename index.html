Ruby Johnston · Founder

People remember how a room made them feel.

A Master’s in Rhetoric, years teaching it at university, now applied to physical space: light, sound, taste, and texture all communicate.

As Director of Experience at Margolis, a 3.6× average sales increase in just over a year. Jubyr LLC, founded 2026, takes it beyond one venue.

01Everything communicates.

Nothing in a room is neutral. Silence, waiting, pricing, lighting, music, the temperature of a greeting — every detail teaches people how seriously you take them. Behavior first, aesthetics second.

02Memory is designed.

Great experiences emerge from hundreds of intentional decisions. Consistency builds trust; surprise builds delight. Guests rarely notice good operations, but always notice bad ones.

03Belonging is the point.

Don’t ask who your customer is — ask who feels comfortable becoming one. People support what they help create.

Services

Seven ways we work.

01Experience design behavior before aesthetics

From a neighborhood cocktail bar to a multi-room music venue, the work begins with human behavior.

Guest journey mapping, environmental strategy, programming concepts, behavioral design, experience audits, creative direction.

02Hospitality consulting hospitality is a language

Service systems, operational efficiency, menu development, staff culture, programming, guest retention, and long-term positioning.

03Venue strategy known for more than what you sell

Your venue’s cultural role in the city, recurring programming, community relationships, operations, and nights people seek out.

04Event production connection over attendance

We start with purpose, not ticket sales. Why should this event exist? Who should leave different than when they arrived?

05Creative direction one cohesive system

Brand partnerships to physical environments — one creative system across design, programming, communication, and guest experience.

06Speaking & education workshops and talks

Experience design, hospitality, rhetoric, behavioral psychology, community building, and the future of cultural spaces — for hospitality teams, universities, and leadership.

07Design Studio custom sites & tools

The same philosophy, applied to software: workflow home pages, client books and rosters, event ops tools — designed beginning to end.
