How the kitchen runs
  1. You buy all your ingredients. Leftovers are yours to keep, freeze or use the next night.
  2. We supply the pie boxes and salad containers.
  3. Every sale rings on our register. No cash, no other register. Sales go to Jubyr LLC.
  4. Sales cover your guaranteed pay, your food money back, boxes, card fees, gas, power and the kitchen.
Ground rules
  1. Who. You run prep, cooking, service, inventory and closing the kitchen clean. A Margolis staff member is on site.

  4. Food safety. A current food handler or ServSafe certificate before the trial night.
  5. Equipment. What Margolis buys stays Margolis's. What you bring stays yours.
  6. Ending early. Either of us can cancel the trial with 48 hours notice.
